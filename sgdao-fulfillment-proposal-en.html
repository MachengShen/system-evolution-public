0 · Problem Origin, 3-Layer Naming Architecture & Permanent Non-Token Commitment Formal Institutional Foundation

The Fundamental Question

"When someone spots something they truly desire—whether an intimate Tokyo counter seat that only takes domestic landline calls on release day, or a handmade iron teapot sold cash-only at a Kyoto artisan's workshop—how can they acquire it in reality without friction, scalper markups, or bait-and-switch fraud?"

What looks superficially like "concierge booking" or "cross-border daigou" is in fact one of the sharpest mechanism design blindspots at the boundary between digital intelligence and physical institutions: once an item crosses outside standardized e-commerce SKUs into the non-standard long tail, pure LLM agents die at the API-free physical wall ($\kappa_{\text{API}}=\infty$), while traditional human broker markets collapse into Akerlof's Lemon Market under hidden action and hidden quality.

Executive Summary (Plain-Language 4 Pillars)

We resolve real-world non-standard acquisition into four plain-language pillars: (1) 5-Factor Additive Logit (Shapley-Style) True-Taste De-Biasing: normalizing harsh connoisseur $z$-scores, orthogonally stripping "grumpy master / strict rules / cramped Showa shop" service penalties (one-sided correction), and anchoring on visual craft priors; (2) EIG-Threshold Hybrid Decomposition $\mathcal{D}(I)$: an illustrative target split (~95% / ~5%) where $\$0$-marginal-cost agents absorb ~95% of digital friction while isolating the ~5% irreducible physical last mile into $\$5\text{–}\$25$ human micro-bounties; (3) 3-Path Orthogonal Verification (pairwise error correlation target $\rho_{ab}<0.2$) + Soulbound Credit $W_i(t)$: staged escrow that makes honest fulfillment incentive-compatible under model assumptions (Proposition 2); and (4) Spectral-Gap ($\Delta\lambda$) & Conductance Governance: monitoring low-conductance cuts ($\lambda_2 \to 0$) on the interaction graph to quarantine collusion rings and bipartite eigengaps ($\Delta\lambda_d$, $\sin\theta \le 0.35$) to spawn specialized agents.

Dual-Loop Capital Metabolism

The system operates without any speculative token via two strictly separated fiat loops: Loop 1 (System Evolution & Cold-Start Calibration) is bounded by a hard $\$100/\text{mo}$ seed pool allocated by Expected Information Gain per dollar ($\text{EIG}(b)/\text{price}(b) \ge \theta_{\text{month}}$); Loop 2 (External Buyer Fulfillment) is $100\%$ self-funded per order via buyer fiat pre-authorization $\to$ 3-path $R_{\text{verified}}$ atomic release $\to$ $3\%\text{–}8\%$ Reality Royalty ($\phi_{\text{royalty}}=0$ on any failed fulfillment).

1 · The Non-Standard Real-World Fulfillment Trilemma Formal Impossibility Theorem & Structural Escape

Let a non-standard acquisition request $e$ be represented as a directed acyclic subtask graph $G_e = (E, \prec)$. For each subtask $s \in E$, define its pure digital API reachability cost $\kappa_{\text{API}}(s) \in [0, \infty]$: when no public digital interface can complete $s$ (e.g., domestic landline callback confirmation, early-morning paper ticket queuing, or cash-only workshop pickup with macro glaze inspection), $\kappa_{\text{API}}(s) = \infty$.

$$ \textbf{(1) Long-Tail Physical Reach:}\quad \mathcal{R}_{\text{phys}}(e) = \Pr\!\Big[\text{Fulfilled}(e) \;\Big|\; \exists\, s \in E:\ \kappa_{\text{API}}(s) = \infty\Big] \ge 1 - \delta $$ $$ \textbf{(2) Zero Principal-Agent Moral Hazard:}\quad \mathcal{M}_{\text{trust}} = \mathbb{E}\big[L_{\text{MH}}\big] = \mathbb{E}\big[\text{Buyer loss from hidden action } a \in \{\text{fake receipt, swap, markup, flight}\}\big] \to 0 $$ $$ \textbf{(3) Zero Frontend Cognitive Load:}\quad \mathcal{C}_{\text{UI}}(e) = K_{\text{interact}}(e) \le \bar{c} \quad (\text{at most 1 swipe/tap + optional 1-tap final visual check}) $$
Proposition 1 · The Single-Homogeneous-Layer Impossibility Trilemma

Statement: Under Assumptions A1 (Physical API Discontinuity $\exists s: \kappa_{\text{API}}(s)=\infty$), A2 (Positive Single-Channel Verification Fragility $f_k \ge \underline{f} > 0$), and A3 (Bounded Buyer Attention $\mathcal{C}_{\text{UI}} \le \bar{c}$), no single-layer homogeneous mechanism can simultaneously achieve $\mathcal{R}_{\text{phys}} \ge 1-\delta$, $\mathbb{E}[L_{\text{MH}}] \le \epsilon$, and $\mathcal{C}_{\text{UI}} \le \bar{c}$:

  • Pure Digital LLM Agents satisfy (2) and (3), but whenever $\exists s \in E$ with $\kappa_{\text{API}}(s)=\infty$, $\mathcal{R}_{\text{phys}}(e) = 0$.
  • Unverified Human Daigou / Freelance Platforms satisfy (1), but under single-channel self-reported receipts ($f_1 \ge \underline{f}$), any agent with cheating temptation $G_{\text{cheat}} > f_1 S_{\text{slash}}$ strictly prefers hidden markup or counterfeit substitution, driving the market into an Akerlof Lemon Equilibrium.
  • Buyer-Managed Direct Sourcing satisfies (1) and (2) only by pushing cross-language calls, price verification, and logistics tracking onto the buyer, violating $\mathcal{C}_{\text{UI}} \le \bar{c}$ by an order of magnitude.

2.2 `C-Pred` × Kiro 5-Factor Additive Logit (Shapley-Style) Score De-Biasing Model v1.0 & Scalper Guard

Public ratings $S_{i,\text{pub}} \in [3.00, 5.00]$ on dining and craft platforms confound culinary execution with reviewer leniency, social etiquette penalties, and platform Bayesian shrinkage. Co-designed with Kiro (`claude-opus-5.5`), our 5-Factor Additive Logit (Shapley-Style) De-Biasing Model maps bounded scores to logit-odds space $\eta_i = \log\frac{S_i - 2.80}{5.00 - S_i}$ and attributes five verifiable components to estimate the counterfactual True-Taste Score $\widehat{S}_i^{\star}$ (currently demonstrated as a heuristic initial calibration on a 31-venue dataset, with full DML cross-fitting in progress):

$$ \widehat{S}_i^{\star} = S_{i,\text{pub}} + \underbrace{\Delta_i^{\text{sample}}}_{\substack{\text{👥 Harsh-Critic } z\text{-Score}\\\text{& Lunch/Dinner Split}}} + \underbrace{\Delta_i^{\text{ortho}}}_{\substack{\text{😤 Grumpy Master / Strict Rules}\\\text{& Cramped Showa Halo Stripped}}} + \underbrace{\Delta_i^{\text{surprise}}}_{\substack{\text{🤯 Review Semantic Surprise}\\(\text{Expectation-Gap Residual})}} + \underbrace{\Delta_i^{\text{vision}}}_{\substack{\text{🔪 Multi-Modal Craft Priors}\\(\text{Blade Edge / Maillard / Shari})}} - \underbrace{\Delta_i^{\text{hype}}}_{\substack{\text{🛡️ Inner-Circle Mutual-Praise}\\\text{& Instagram-Bait Discount}}} $$
  • 1. $\Delta_i^{\text{sample}}$ (Reviewer $z$-Score & Sample Niche Correction): Normalizes each reviewer by historical mean $\mu_u$ and spread $\sigma_u$ ($z_{iu}=(r_{iu}-\mu_u)/\sigma_u$) and separates cheap lunch-set drag from evening omakase execution.
  • 2. $\Delta_i^{\text{ortho}}$ (One-Sided Orthogonal Stripping of Service/Vibe Penalties): Applies DML-style residualization (planned; currently a one-sided heuristic correction) to strip out non-culinary penalties caused by taciturn artisan masters (muaiso / ganko taisho), strict counter rules (no perfume, no loud chatter), or cramped Showa interiors—triggered strictly when food subscore exceeds service subscore by $r_{iu}^{\text{food}} - r_{iu}^{\text{srv}} \ge 0.4$.
  • 3. $\Delta_i^{\text{surprise}}$ (Semantic Expectation-Gap Residual): Extracts conditional surprise from 1st-hand Japanese reviews ("Ginza ¥30k quality hiding at 3.4x" vs. "overhyped photo-bait").
  • 4. $\Delta_i^{\text{vision}}$ (Multi-Modal Relative Craft Metrics): Evaluates category-relative physical execution (Kohada crosshatch blade geometry, binchotan Maillard crust uniformity, shari grain integrity, collagen broth emulsion) while penalizing gold-leaf/dry-ice gimmicks.
  • 5. $-\Delta_i^{\text{hype}}$ (Inner-Circle Mutual-Praise Discount & Honest Chain Cap): Penalizes high-density reviewer clique co-occurrence and strictly enforces Kiro's Honest Chain Discipline—mass-market chains (PRONTO, Tengu, Wan) are capped within $\pm 0.08$ ($3.35\to 3.41\text{–}3.44$), never falsely inflated to 3.8 kaiseki territory.

2.3 Optimal Hybrid Decomposition Operator $\mathcal{D}(I)$ (Information-Value Gate)

For each subtask $s \in E$, $\mathcal{D}(I)$ assigns executor $x_s \in \{\mathsf{A}\text{ (Agent)},\,\mathsf{H}\text{ (Human)}\}$ (targeting an illustrative target split (~95% / ~5%) between digital agent subtasks and physical last-mile micro-bounties). A human micro-bounty is awakened if and only if:

$$ x_s = \mathsf{H} \iff \kappa_{\text{API}}(s) = \infty \quad \lor \quad \text{EIG}_{\text{phys}}(s) > \tau_{\text{gate}}(s),\qquad \tau_{\text{gate}}(s) = \frac{b_s - \text{VOI}_{\mathsf{A}}(s)}{\nu} $$

where $\text{EIG}_{\text{phys}}(s) = H(Y) - \mathbb{E}_{o\sim p(o\mid s)}[H(Y\mid o)]$ is the expected mutual information gain from physical human observation over latent fulfillment quality $Y$, $\nu$ is the monetary loss avoided per nat of uncertainty reduction, and $b_s \in [\$5, \$25]$ is the micro-bounty cost.

3 · Three-Path Orthogonal Verification ($R_{\text{verified}}$) & Staged Fiat Escrow Collapsing Principal-Agent Moral Hazard

To prevent fake reservations, counterfeit craft substitution, or hidden markups, the proposed staged escrow mechanism requires three verification signals $r = (r_A, r_B, r_C) \in \{0,1\}^3$ from physically and institutionally orthogonal channels with a pairwise error correlation target $\rho_{ab} < 0.2$:

Verification Path Physical / Institutional Signal Source (Proposed Architecture) What It Proves & Single-Path Fragility $f_k$
Path A · Institutional Receipt
r_A ∈ {0,1}		 Attested telecom call log ($\ge 90\text{s}$ to merchant landline) + merchant confirmation SMS/receipt, or workshop written hold slip. Proves institutional contact occurred ($f_A \le 0.08$).
Path B · Live Sensory Nonce Probe
r_B ∈ {0,1}		 Continuous macro video displaying a real-time cryptographic challenge nonce alongside the craft item's base hallmark + knuckle-tap acoustic resonance spectrum (proposed). Defeats replay attacks, screen-recording spoofs, and cracked/molded counterfeits ($f_B \le 0.06$).
Path C · Downstream Handshake
r_C ∈ {0,1}		 Buyer geo-fenced seat check-in at restaurant, or tamper-evident numbered seal applied on camera + EMS official scale weight match ($\le \pm 50\text{g}$). Eliminates post-video box-swapping before shipment ($f_C \le 0.05$).
Proposition 2 · Joint False-Acceptance Bound & Incentive-Compatible Honesty under Model Assumptions HEURISTIC / MODEL DERIVATION
$$ \Pr\big[R_{\text{verified}} = 1 \mid \text{Fraud}\big] \;\le\; \prod_{k \in \{A,B,C\}} f_k + \sum_{a < b} \rho_{ab}\sqrt{f_a(1-f_a)f_b(1-f_b)} $$ $$ \textbf{Incentive-Compatibility Condition (Proposition 2):}\qquad G_{\text{cheat}}(V_{\text{order}}) \;\le\; \big(1 - P_{\text{FA}}\big)\Big(D_i + \gamma \cdot \text{NPV}\big(W_i(t)\big)\Big) - c_{\text{effort}} $$

With illustrative single-path fragilities $(f_A, f_B, f_C) = (0.08, 0.06, 0.05)$: under full conditional independence ($\rho_{ab} = 0$), joint false acceptance collapses to $\prod_k f_k = 2.4 \times 10^{-4}$ ($>99.9\%$ detection, $\ll \min_k f_k$); even at the upper pairwise correlation bound $\rho_{ab} = 0.2$, the conservative additive bound yields $P_{\text{FA}} \le 0.0353$ ($\ge 96.5\%$ single-order detection). Because proposed Soulbound Track-Record weight $W_i(t)$ updates with asymmetric decay ($\eta_- \ll \eta_+$: slow to build, instant slashing to zero upon verified fraud) and caps order exposure via $V_{\text{order}} \le \kappa W_i(t) + D_i$, honest fulfillment is incentive-compatible under model assumptions (Proposition 2), forfeiting both the cash bond $D_i$ and the present value of future bounty flows upon detection.

4.1 Spectral-Gap ($\Delta\lambda$) & Conductance Governance: Collusion Defense vs. Specialist Emergence

$$ L_{\text{norm}} = I - D^{-1/2} A D^{-1/2},\qquad \Delta\lambda_d = \lambda_{k+1} - \lambda_k \ge \tau_{\text{gap}},\qquad \sin\theta(U_k, \hat{U}_k) \le \frac{\lVert \hat{E} \rVert_2}{\Delta\lambda_d} \le 0.35 $$

We apply two distinct spectral diagnostics across two different graphs: (1) Specialist Agent Emergence on the Task-Capability Bipartite Graph: when a subtask cluster exhibits a stable eigengap $\Delta\lambda_d$ where the Davis–Kahan eigenvector perturbation bound $\sin\theta \le 0.35$ is used as a stability gate and accumulates $\ge 40\%$ of verified receipts $R_{\text{verified}}$, the mesh spawns a dedicated specialist agent (`Tabelog-Booker`, `Artisan-Sourcing-Scout`, `Concierge-Negotiator`, `Quality-Inspector`). (2) Collusion Quarantine on the Reviewer-Runner Interaction Graph: isolated collusion cliques with weak external links and anomalous internal mutual-rating density manifest as a low-conductance cluster (near-zero Fiedler eigenvalue $\lambda_2 \to 0$), triggering immediate quarantine.

7 · Four Open Peer-Review Questions for Friends & Critics Stress-Testing the Mechanism

Q1 · Cold-Start Runner Liquidity in Low-Density Craft Towns OPEN MECHANISM QUESTION

In rural craft regions (e.g., Chiang Mai or Hokuriku kiln villages), strict exposure caps $V_{\text{order}} \le \kappa W_i(t) + D_i$ prevent new runners with low initial $W_0$ from taking $\$200$ craft orders when few small phone bounties exist locally. Should the $\$100/\text{mo}$ seed pool subsidize initial $W_0$, boost honest-attempt fees $a_0$, or allow Web-of-Trust vouching where veteran nodes stake their own $W_i$?

Q2 · Path B Analog-Hole Spoofing & Merchant-Runner Collusion OPEN SECURITY QUESTION

When a runner and a dishonest shop clerk collude to swap an item in a camera blind spot after filming the real piece, error correlation $\rho_{AB}$ spikes. Beyond continuous single-take tamper-seal filming + acoustic tap fingerprinting + EMS scale weight matching ($\pm 50\text{g}$), what lightweight physical invariant best restores orthogonality without requiring a destination-port blind inspector (Path D)?

Q3 · Actuarial Pricing of Post-Path-A Merchant Default OPEN ACTUARIAL QUESTION

If a runner honestly completes Path A reservation/hold, but the external merchant later cancels unilaterally, the protocol pays the runner and refunds the buyer ($\phi=0$). Should high-default categories carry an actuarial risk premium inside $\phi_{\text{royalty}}(c)$, or be pruned from `C-Res`?

Q4 · Dynamic AI Voice vs. Human Concierge Boundary $\tau_{\text{gate}}$ & Merchant Goodwill OPEN GAME-THEORY QUESTION

Concurrent automated AI voice calls risk triggering industry-wide merchant backlash (Tragedy of the Commons). Should the objective function explicitly price "Merchant Goodwill Depletion" and mandate native human micro-bounties for top artisan venues?
