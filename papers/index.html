How to index / cite us

System Evolution Open Preprints is an arXiv-style open-access preprint series with stable, permanent identifiers of the form SE-YYYY.NNNNN. Every paper in this series ships with a typeset archival PDF (compiled via papers/arxiv-print.css), an interactive first-principles HTML treatise with live numerical simulations, cryptographic SHA-256 checksums for integrity verification, and a standard BibTeX citation record.

Automated indexers, academic crawlers, and AI agent clients can ingest our machine-readable catalog at papers/catalog.json and repository-level citation metadata at CITATION.cff (alongside llms.txt). All works are self-hosted and distributed under Apache-2.0 / CC-BY-4.0 open licenses.
